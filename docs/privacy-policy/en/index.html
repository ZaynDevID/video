Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Last updated 3 November 2026

In short: Video is a local/offline video player. The app has no account, ads, analytics, or internet permission. Your video files and library data stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

1. Who operates this app

Video (package com.zayndev.video) is developed and published by ZaynDev. Privacy questions can be sent using the Developer email button on the app's official Google Play listing.

2. Data the app accesses

  • Video files on your device. With your permission, the app reads video files from device storage so it can display and play them.
  • App settings. Theme, sort order, and display preferences are stored locally.
  • Watch history. The last position of each video is stored locally so Continue watching works. You can clear it from the More menu.
  • Selected subtitles. If you choose a subtitle file, the app reads it only from the location you selected and displays it during playback.
  • Crash logs. If the app encounters an error, technical details (including device model and Android version) may be written to the app folder and/or Downloads/Video. These logs are not sent automatically and remain on your device.

The app does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or browsing history.

3. Permissions used

  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO (Android 13+) and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below): read video files.
  • READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED (Android 14+): allow access only to the videos you select when using limited access.

Permissions are requested only when needed and can be revoked at any time in Android settings.

4. Collection and sharing

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data. The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics, tracking, or third-party services that transmit data. No data is sent to our servers because the app has no internet connection.

5. Storage and deletion

Settings and watch history are stored on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Subtitle files are read only for the playback session; their contents are not copied. Original video files are not modified or deleted by the app. Crash logs in Downloads/Video can be deleted with a file manager.

6. Security

Because data is not sent off the device, risk depends mainly on your device security. We recommend keeping a screen lock and backing up important video files separately.

7. Children

This app is not specifically directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. It only plays video files already on the device or selected by the user.

8. Links to other sites

The Privacy Policy menu opens this page in a browser. If you open another link, that site's privacy policy applies. The app does not send data to the browser.

9. Changes to this policy

If data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. The latest version is always available at this address.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, corrections, or requests, use the Developer email button on the Video Google Play listing. We will respond through the channel available there.
